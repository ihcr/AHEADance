How it works

Listen. Improvise. Dance.

One model, listening as the song plays. Nobody tells it the style or the tempo: it hears them in the music and dances them. Every performance is improvised, so the same song becomes a new dance each time.

  1. 1 · Listen

    It hears the style.

    AHEADance listens to the music as it plays, from a file or through a microphone, and hears its style, rhythm and energy for itself.

  2. 2 · Improvise

    A new dance every time.

    From what it hears, it creates the robot's next moves for all 29 joints, thirty times a second, even for songs it has never heard.

  3. 3 · Dance

    On a real humanoid.

    The robot dances while the music plays. When the music stops, it settles back to standing; when the music returns, it dances again.

Send us up to 30 s of music and we add the robot's dance to this list. How it works

Play a music file here. Our model on a cloud GPU listens as it plays, and the robot dances to it, as on the real robot. Pause the music and the robot settles; play it again and the robot dances again. It learned from music only: talking or noise makes it move gently, and silence makes it stand. The robot you see is the motion tracker our robot runs, following the model's motion in a simulator next to it. It is shown a little after the generated motion; switch to Generated motion to see that sooner.

To go live, sign in with your email under Your song: 2 sessions a day, up to 1 minute each.

    • One run, exactly this audio.Every dance in the web demo is one run of the deployed model on the audio you hear: seed 1234, except the three Same song takes (seeds 1234, 1235, 1236) and Live (a new seed each session). Nothing is cut together from other runs.
    • Tracker or generated motion.By default a replay shows the generated motion followed by the whole-body motion tracker our robot runs, in MuJoCo physics, so the robot's own collisions and balance apply. Generated motion shows what the model output, as it is. Neither is the joint angles measured on the real robot. Live shows the tracker too. If the tracker falls or stops, the page says so and shows the generated motion. The songs you send show the generated motion.
    • Robot status.The label on the stage comes from the joint speed of that motion: below 0.12 rad/s is standing, above 0.25 rad/s is dancing.
    • Styles are only labels.Style names are the FineDance music labels. The model only hears the music.
